Our story

La Grange Montante is owned by Tony & Gill

We want you to have a wonderful holiday. The gîte is next to our house, so if you need us, we're on hand to advise and help you get the most from your stay.

Two lives, one dream in the Dordogne

We both love France, and we love the Dordogne in particular. After working for several years in the hospitality and service industries, we moved to France to fulfil our dream, and to set up a gîte business of our own.

Every stay starts with the same hope: that you leave feeling like you've had a proper rest, in a place that's genuinely looked after. That's easier when your hosts live right next door.

/ The gîte
The gîte

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A comfortable, well-equipped stone gîte with its own pool, open all year round.

Our gîte comfortably sleeps two people in a double bed on the mezzanine level. A double sofa bed in the lounge means three or four guests can be accommodated, and cot and camp bed facilities are available on request.

The lounge/dining area has a TV and DVD player, Wifi, a wood-burning stove and electric radiators. The fully equipped kitchen includes an electric hob, microwave/steam oven, washing machine, dishwasher and coffee maker, plus a separate fridge and freezer. There's a shower room with downstairs toilet and heated towel rail. Ironing board, iron and hairdryer included.

Externally there's a seating area, pergola and BBQ, with exclusive use of the pool during your stay, and free on-site parking.

Smoking and pets are not permitted in the gîte.
