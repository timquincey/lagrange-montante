The gîte

A converted barn that sleeps up to four

La Grange Montante is a holiday gîte situated in the Périgord-Limousin Regional Park, in the Dordogne region of France. The converted barn, which would ideally suit three people, but can sleep up to four, is situated in a quiet hamlet.

Sleeps 2–4 A double bed on the mezzanine, plus a double sofa bed for three or four. Cot and camp bed facilities are also available.
Private pool Exclusive use of the pool during your stay, plus a seating area / pergola and BBQ.

The area

The Périgord-Limousin Regional Park, on your doorstep

Just 15 minutes from the town of Nontron, it is an ideal location for those who would like to relax. Alternatively, for those who wish to be more active, within less than two hours you can access the towns of Bergerac, Cognac, Sarlat, Périgueux, Limoges and Angoulême.

  • Piégut weekly market10 min
  • Nontron town & weekly market15 min
  • Cognac & its vineyards1 hr
  • Bergerac vineyards1.5 hrs

Feeling more active? Go cycling (bikes available), lake swimming, canoeing or karting, all within a short distance.

/ The gîte
The gîte

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A comfortable, well-equipped stone gîte with its own pool, open all year round.

Our gîte is located in a converted barn, which is next to our house.

Our new gîte comfortably sleeps two people in a double bed on the mezzanine level. However, there is a double sofa bed in the lounge area, so three or four people could be accommodated. Cot and camp bed facilities are also available.

There is a lounge / dining area, with TV and DVD player, Wifi, wood-burning stove, and electric radiators. There is a fully equipped kitchen, with electric hob, microwave / steam oven, washing machine, dishwasher and coffee maker. There is a separate fridge and freezer. There is a shower room with downstairs toilet and heated towel rail. An ironing board, iron and hairdryer are included.

Externally there is a seating area / pergola and BBQ. Guests have exclusive use of the pool during their stay. Free parking is available on site.

The gîte is available all the year round. Why not spend Christmas / New Year in this wonderful part of France?

We do not allow smoking in the gîte, and unfortunately pets are also not allowed.
