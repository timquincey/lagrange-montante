/ The gîte
The gîte

The gîte

Our well-equipped stone gîte, with its own private pool.

Our converted-barn stone gîte comfortably sleeps two people in a double bed on the mezzanine level. There is a double sofa bed in the lounge area, so three or four people could be accommodated. Cot and camp bed facilities are also available.

There is a lounge / dining area, with TV and DVD player, Wifi, wood-burning stove, and electric radiators. There is a fully equipped kitchen, with electric hob, microwave / steam oven, washing machine, dishwasher and coffee maker. There is a separate fridge and freezer. There is a shower room with downstairs toilet and heated towel rail. An ironing board, iron and hairdryer are included.

Externally there is a seating area / pergola and BBQ. Guests have exclusive use of the pool during their stay.

Free parking is available on site.

The gîte is available all the year round. Why not spend Christmas / New Year in this wonderful part of France?

We do not allow smoking in the gîte, and unfortunately pets are also not allowed.

Rental costs

We have a very simple pricing structure!

The cost of the gîte is £880 / €1,050 per week, whenever you would like to come and stay!

We prefer Saturday to Saturday bookings. However, if this does not fit with your own arrangements, then please get in touch with us to check availability.

  • 20% of the rental costs (£176 / €210) are payable on confirmation of the booking, with the balance due one month before your stay.
  • The above rental costs includes heating, bed linen, gîte towels and separate pool towels, and is also fully inclusive of cleaning costs. So there are no hidden extra costs!
  • A local tourist tax, levied by the French government, is payable at the rate of 1.5 euros per person, per night.

/ Getting here
Getting here

Getting here

By car or by plane… the choice is yours.

By car

Depending on where you live in the UK, you can take any of the main southern ports to France. Folkestone and Dover to Calais gives you the choice between the ferry and Le Shuttle.

Typical driving times from the northern French ports are:

Caen / St Malo
6 hours
Dieppe
5 hours
Calais
8 hours

If you choose to come by car, then we would be happy to let you have detailed directions. Alternatively just type La Grange Montante into Google Maps, and it will bring you to our door!

Many of our clients stay overnight ‘half-way’ from the ports to us (especially from Calais). We can advise on some good places to stay if you are thinking of taking this option.

By plane

We are just under an hour from Limoges Airport, and one and a half hours from Bergerac Airport.

Flights are more frequent, and from more UK airports during the summer.

To Limoges flights from the UK operate all year round from Stansted, Manchester and East Midlands. In the summer there are also flights from Gatwick, Leeds / Bradford and Bristol.

To Bergerac flights do not always operate during the winter, but in the summer there are flights from Gatwick and Stansted.

Car hire is available from both airports.
