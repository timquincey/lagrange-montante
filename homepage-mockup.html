Meet your hosts

Tony & Gill have called this corner of the Dordogne home since they swapped hospitality careers for a gîte of their own.

The gîte sits right next to their house, so if you need anything during your stay — restaurant tips, a spare bottle of gas, directions to the Saturday market — they're on hand to help. You'll almost certainly meet Barney and Gillie, their two Shetland ponies, and Petrus the dog, on your way to the pool.
